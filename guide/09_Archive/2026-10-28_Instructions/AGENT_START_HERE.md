Use the formal proposal for project objectives, the unchanged Word guide for the supplied experiment, and the context notes for the user's intent. Generated simulation notes are proposals. Preserve source conflicts and resolve them explicitly. Never promote an old assistant suggestion into an approved experimental fact.

The primary simulator relationship is **resulting structure → resulting properties**. Keep material state (dry versus resin-filled), axes, units, composition basis and measurement conditions with every parameter. Unknown values are null in the input template.

Do not treat visual HTML demos as validated simulation. Do not claim that all paper PDFs or the recording transcript are included: consult the availability register and pending-transcript note. Keep complete future transcripts intact; put interpretations in separate files.

Before reporting predictions, provide analytic verification, mesh/time convergence evidence, energy balance, parameter provenance and comparison with relevant experimental data. Missing material data can be explored as labelled sensitivity scenarios.
