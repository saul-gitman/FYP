The review covers aerogel structure, nanoscale solid and gas transport, gas–solid coupling, radiation, effective-conductivity model families and test methods. It highlights that the measurement arrangement and internal radiation can affect the inferred conductivity.

**Implementation use (editor inference):** use this as the conceptual starting reference, then select a specific, documented submodel for the intended material and scale. It is not a complete software specification or measured-property dataset for CNF/CNT/BN/MTMS. Pair it with Obori's CNF model, directional BN/polymer research and the added N-series papers. Keep dry, resin-infiltrated and complete-laminate states separate.
