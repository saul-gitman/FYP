The study separates gas and solid domains and uses lattice Boltzmann modelling with interpolated subdomain boundary temperatures. It examines how phase length scales affect temperature jumps and coupled conductivity.

**Implementation use (editor inference):** a research reference for extending a simple effective-medium model to resolved gas–solid transport and for testing scale sensitivity. It is not a requirement to use LBM in the first version. Its model geometry and nanoscale assumptions must be checked before adapting it to aligned CNF/BN or CNT-containing structures. Do not import its parameters as project measurements.
