This study perfuses h-BN into a porous CNF scaffold to form conducting paths in a composite film. It reports through-plane conductivity of 1.488 W/(m K) at 9.51 vol% h-BN for that processed film.

**Implementation use (editor inference):** compare this material state with dry insulating aerogels to make clear why the simulator must track connectivity, processing and remaining pores. The reported value is not a conductivity input for the present dry aerogel or cured CFRP interlayer. It is not an epoxy-infusion validation study and does not establish the effect of this project's resin uptake.
