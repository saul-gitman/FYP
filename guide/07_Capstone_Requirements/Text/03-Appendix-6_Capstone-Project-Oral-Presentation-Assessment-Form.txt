AAE4002/AAE4012 Capstone Project 

Oral Presentation Assessment 

(For the use by Members of the Oral Presentation Panel)

The Oral Presentation component of the assessment methods is 30 marks in total. The Oral Presentation is assessed by a panel. The mark given by each panel member has equal weight.

The assessment methods and the associated marks of AAE4002/AAE4012 are shown in Table 1.

Table 1: Assessment methods and the associated marks

 | Assessments | Marks

1 | Interim Report | 20

2 | Final Report | 50

3 | Oral Presentation | 30

 | Total | 100



Each group has to submit a soft copy of the oral presentation powerpoint for the department’s record.
